# Voice and tone — player-facing text

All text a player can see MUST be written in **archaic, high-fantasy English** — the old, mystical register of a magical world. This is a hard requirement for every feature, present and future.

## The register (level: moderate)
Aim for an old, ceremonious, slightly mystical voice that is still easy to read. Evocative, not a grammar puzzle.

- DO lean on archaic/elevated diction and cadence: `hark`, `thus`, `whence`, `beyond`, `the old roads`, `I bid thee`, `come`, `heed`, `thy path`, `a boon`, `seek`, `tarry`, `yonder`.
- DO use `thee / thou / thy / thine` and old verb forms (`hast`, `doth`, `art`, `'tis`) **sparingly, for flavor** — a touch per passage, not every sentence.
- DO keep sentences understandable at a glance. If a line needs re-reading to parse, it is too heavy — pull it back.
- DON'T write full Early-Modern-English grammar everywhere (no relentless `thou goest / thou hast not`), and don't invent broken pseudo-archaic grammar. When unsure, prefer a clear, formal, old-sounding line over a dense one.

## Scope — what changes
- **Everything the player reads:** story/prologue lines, the ready/resume/decline gate lines, `/invite` wording, shop messages, `/daily`, `/collection`, `/card`, `/profile`, `/battle`, `/admin` output, and all error/notice strings (closed-DM, "check your DMs", insufficient coins, not found, etc.).
- **Button labels** (e.g. Continue, Yes/No, Open the book, Buy): archaic voice too.
- **Slash-command descriptions:** lightly archaic is fine.

## Scope — what stays
- **Slash-command names** stay plain modern English (`/story`, `/shop`, `/daily`, …) — players type them and Discord constrains naming.
- **Proper nouns stay exactly as-is:** the Vietnamese names (e.g. `Bò Tuôi`) and the setting's names (`The Eyes`, `Stranger Eyes`, `The Eyes Of Wisdom`).
- Keep the established single-stranger direct-address voice in the story (speak to the player as "thou/you"); hidden speaker label stays.

## Examples (moderate archaic)
- "Check your DMs" → "Look to thy private messages — we shall speak there."
- "You don't have enough coins." → "Thy purse falls short, traveler."
- "Card not found." → "No such card is known to me."
- "Continue" (button) → "Onward"
- "Open the book" (button) → "Open the tome"
